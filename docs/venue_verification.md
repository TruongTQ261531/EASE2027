## SIGSOFT policy source checked separately

Read the [SIGSOFT policy repository](https://github.com/acmsigsoft/open-science-policies/blob/master/sigsoft-open-science-policies.md),
a general venue blueprint, not a filled-in 2027 track-specific policy. It asks for a Data Availability
section after Conclusion, encourages anonymized review artifacts and preserved open releases with
appropriate licenses, and distinguishes preserved archives from GitHub alone. Do not turn its
encouragement into a mandatory public-before-review requirement. CFP itself requires compliance
and anonymous supplements. A guessed sigsoft.org policy URL returned404; the repository text
